    Selected work

    • Enterprise RAG over policy documents

      At Carelon I built a retrieval system on LLaMA 70B, LangGraph, and Qdrant with cross-encoder reranking, covering 25+ policy documents for 200+ internal users. Top-1 accuracy went from 66% to 78%, and P95 retrieval latency dropped from 1.8s to 0.9s.

      See it on my resume

    • Multi-agent system for member requests

      A LangGraph and GPT-4o system coordinating four service agents for address updates, ID cards, member inquiries, and policy terminations. It handled 500+ requests a day at an 85% straight-through rate, with human-in-the-loop validation.

      See it on my resume

    • Published research on feasible production plans

      My paper in Chemical Engineering Transactions (2023) trains SVM and Random Forest classifiers to separate feasible steel production plans from infeasible ones, then turns the SVM boundary into a single plane equation a planner can check without re-running the model.
